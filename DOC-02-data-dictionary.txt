DATA DICTIONARY

DATA-01-oop-panel-2013-2024.csv
  114 rows, one per lipid-lowering class and year, 2013 to 2024. National rows of
  the CMS Medicare Part D Prescribers by Geography and Drug annual public use files.
    year                      calendar year
    agent_class               lipid-lowering class, 11 values
    tier                      working label grouping classes by cost per fill; unused
    fills                     standardized 30-day fill count, fractional because CMS
                              standardizes days supply to 30-day equivalents
    benes                     beneficiaries with at least one fill
    spend                     gross spending, US dollars, all payers combined
    lis_oop                   beneficiary cost sharing among enrollees receiving the
                              low-income subsidy (LIS_Bene_Cst_Shr)
    nonlis_oop                beneficiary cost sharing among enrollees not receiving
                              the subsidy (NonLIS_Bene_Cst_Shr)
    tot_oop_per_fill          (lis_oop + nonlis_oop) / fills, pooled across groups
    pct_oop_borne_by_nonLIS   100 * nonlis_oop / (lis_oop + nonlis_oop)

  Class labels, panel value to manuscript label:
    PCSK9 monoclonal              PCSK9 mAb
    Inclisiran (siRNA)            Inclisiran
    Bempedoic acid                Bempedoic acid
    Icosapent ethyl               Icosapent ethyl
    Niacin (Rx)                   Niacin
    Ezetimibe (alone)             Ezetimibe
    Fibrate                       Fibrate
    Statin (incl. combinations)   Statin (including combinations)

  Present in the panel but not reported in the letter: Bile acid sequestrant,
  Prescription omega-3 (other), Other orphan (ANGPTL3/MTP/ASO).

  Inclisiran first appears in 2021 and bempedoic acid in 2020, so those classes
  present in each year.

DATA-02-lis-enrollment-2013-2025.csv
  Part D enrollment by subsidy status, from the CMS Medicare Monthly Enrollment file.
    year        calendar year
    total       Part D enrollees
    lis         enrollees receiving the low-income subsidy
    no_lis      enrollees not receiving it
    resid       total minus (lis + no_lis); zero or one in every year
    pct_lis, pct_no_lis   percentages of total

DATA-03-national-series-2013-2024.csv
  National utilization series by class and year: beneficiaries, fills, claims,
  gross spending, prescribers, enrollees and users per 1000. Supplies context for
  the trend panel; no reported value depends on it.
    partd_enrollees  sum of the state rows of the enrollment file, which omits a
                     small residual CMS does not assign to a state. It is
                     therefore 11 to 239 lower than DATA-02 total, which is the
                     National row. DATA-02 is the denominator used for every
                     reported ratio.

  The manuscript Table. Carries one extra column, beneficiary_share_of_gross_pct,
  which is derived here but is not reported in the letter and is not checked
  against a printed value.

  One row per reported value: quantity, the figure as printed, the figure
  recomputed from DATA-01 and DATA-02, the tolerance, and PASS or FAIL.

DATA-04-alldrug-national-2013-2024.csv
  Every national row of the Medicare Part D Prescribers by Geography and Drug
  file for each year, all drugs. Columns are the CMS field names: Gnrc_Name,
  Brnd_Name, Tot_30day_Fills, Tot_Clms, Tot_Benes (suppressed below 11),
  Tot_Drug_Cst, LIS_Bene_Cst_Shr, NonLIS_Bene_Cst_Shr, plus year. Comparator
  classes in CODE-03 are defined by generic-name substrings listed in that file.

DATA-05-cpi-u-annual-2013-2024.csv
  year; cpi_u_annual_avg (BLS CPI-U, all items, U.S. city average, not
  seasonally adjusted, annual average, series CUUR0000SA0); cpi_source.

DATA-06-partb-inclisiran-2022-2024.csv
  Inclisiran (HCPCS J1306) from the CMS Medicare Part B Spending by Drug file
  (fee-for-service beneficiaries only; no cost-sharing or subsidy fields):
  partb_total_spending, partb_claims, partb_beneficiaries and derived averages,
  beside the Part D totals for inclisiran from DATA-01 for the same years.
  Part B and Part D cover different populations, so the ratio is a comparison
  of spending captured in the two public files, not a Medicare-wide share.
